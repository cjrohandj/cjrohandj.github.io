Play Harder

Jan 14, 2025

I think we spend too much time in between working and playing. Spending hours on social media, watching tv by ourselves, finishing a bag of chips while staring at the ceiling - I wouldn't classify any of these actions as either play or work, but they take up a lot of time out of my and my friends day. These actions aren't particularly social, they don't help further my career, and I don't go to sleep happy when I spend a good amoutn of my day doing them. The commonly prescribed solution is to work harder, delete instagram, spend more time away from your phone screen and more time on your laptop coding or doing homework.
